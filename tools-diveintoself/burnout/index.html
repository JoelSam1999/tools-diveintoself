Questions

Good to know

Is this a diagnosis?

No. It's a self-reflection tool to help you notice patterns. Only a qualified professional can assess your mental health properly.

Are my answers saved anywhere?

No. Everything happens in your browser. Nothing is stored or sent to us unless you choose to share it.

What is it based on?

The Copenhagen Burnout Inventory (Kristensen et al., 2005), a free and widely used research questionnaire. We've lightly adapted the wording for clarity.

What if my score is high?

You're not alone, and burnout is very treatable. We'll suggest practical steps, and you can book a free 30-minute call with one of our psychologists. If you ever feel unsafe, call Tele-MANAS on 14416.

Your scores

How is this scored?

Each answer is worth 0 to 100, and each area's score is the average of its answers. Researchers commonly treat 50 or more as a sign of significant burnout. We use 0–49 Low, 50–74 Moderate, 75–99 High and 100 Very high. These are guides, not a diagnosis.
